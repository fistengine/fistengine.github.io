1. Introduction

Welcome to FistEngine. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and handled when you visit or interact with the FistEngine.

By using FistEngine, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

FistEngine is designed primarily as a content discovery and download platform. We do not require visitors to create an account simply to browse the website.

Depending on how you use the website, certain technical or usage information may be collected automatically by our website, hosting provider, analytics services, advertising partners, or other third-party services.

Information may include:

  • IP address or approximate location
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type and operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Referring pages
  • Date and time of visits
  • General website usage information

3. Cookies

FistEngine may use cookies and similar technologies to improve website functionality, remember preferences, understand website usage, and support advertising services.

Cookies are small files stored on your device by a website. You can usually control or disable cookies through your browser settings.

4. Advertising

FistEngine may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising networks.

These advertising providers may use cookies, identifiers, or similar technologies to display advertisements and measure advertising performance.

Third-party advertisers may have their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any advertising provider whose services appear on FistEngine.

5. Third-Party Websites and Links

FistEngine may contain links to external websites, download hosts, services, advertisements, or other third-party platforms, but may have different security and privacy practices.

Once you leave FistEngine and visit a third-party website, that website's own privacy policy and terms apply. FistEngine is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or policies of external websites.

6. Downloads and External Files

FistEngine may provide links to applications, games, files, ports, or external download locations.

Users should exercise appropriate caution before downloading or installing any file and should verify compatibility, permissions, file integrity, and the source of the file where appropriate.

7. Children's Privacy

FistEngine does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through our website, please contact us so that the information can be reviewed and appropriate action can be taken.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to maintain the security of information associated with our website. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

FistEngine generally does not intentionally maintain personal user profiles for ordinary visitors. Information collected automatically by hosting, analytics, advertising, or security services may be retained according to the policies and retention practices of those services.

10. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have certain rights regarding personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing of your information.

Requests can be submitted through our contact method provided below. We may need additional information to verify and process a request.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

FistEngine may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the website, services, technologies, or legal requirements change.

Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
